Interpretation and audit boundaries

This is a source-linked recovery, not a claim that the remaining 20 mechanisms now have complete 72-cell audits. A documented exact or partial match is a statement about requirements in an official strategy, policy or action. Publication and approval are evidence of planned responsibilities, not evidence of implementation. An official announcement of actual activity is marked separately; the relevant impact is still unverified.

The current source matrix has 85 theoretical cells, 72 defined interventions and 13 deliberately undefined. Per mechanism, only the identified and source-linked cells are classified. The others are Evidence review outstanding, not "No initiative exists". Prior historical aggregate totals must not be redistributed to individual pillars.

Self-check rule: every credited match has (1) a matrix ID, (2) actual institution, (3) original pillar, (4) official URL, (5) stage, (6) an explicit why/why-not rationale. Approved programmes and separately reported delivery are kept apart.

What must be updated in the existing dashboard?

Country-specific evidence needs to enter the intervention assessment matrix first, including source URL, accountable institution and activity stage. The Case Studies HTML then renders that evidence and recalculates descriptive statistics. The master Bodies & Strategies directory only needs changes when a newer instrument, attribution or document status has been independently established. The existing seven detailed audits should remain untouched until rechecked.

The included CSVs provide machine-readable updates and explain each proposed classification. This companion page does not overwrite your current Case Studies page. An integration script is supplied to append this research section to your current Case_Studies.html while preserving its existing country cards and styling.

What belongs in an effective road safety taskforce?

Ideal evidence framework: 17 government and economic activity domains, five operational Safe System pillars, 85 possible cells and 72 defined intervention themes. This is a proposed checklist, not a claim that every country implements every action.

17Institutional domains
5Safe System pillars
72Defined interventions
13Undefined cells

Each intervention is a candidate requirement for comparison, not a universal assignment of statutory powers. Undefined cells require targeted research, not a negative score.

How to use the checklist

Institutional test: determine the formal and functional representation for each of the 17 domains and cite the legal instrument or credible evidence. Action test: for each of the 72 defined interventions identify the agency accountable for the planned responsibility and compare the source action to the entire ideal requirement. Delivery test: separately document implementation budget, deadlines, completion and outcomes.

X means this ideal matrix cell is intentionally undefined in the supplied framework. N/A in country mapping means available evidence has not established the requirement. Neither should be interpreted as proof that a country has no relevant safety initiative.
Framework basis: supplied COFOG/ISIC workbook, WHO Global Plan, GRSF, UNECE and other citations stored per intervention.

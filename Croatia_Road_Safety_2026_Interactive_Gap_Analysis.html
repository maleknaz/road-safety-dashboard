Executive interpretation

Croatia's Government adopted the National Road Traffic Safety Plan 2021–2030 on 29 July 2021, directing the Ministry of the Interior (MUP) to coordinate implementation. A separate expert committee contributed to formulation in 2020; the minister-appointed implementation working group oversees activities, prioritization, financing proposals, evaluation and reporting. These are different roles.

Assessment rule. The ideal COFOG/ISIC matrix measures coverage of 17 government/economic domains and 72 specified responsibilities. F = a direct formal working-group/ministerial role; P = documented narrower partner or portfolio link; N/A = not verified. For missions A = exact planned or institutionally assigned match; P = partial; N/A = not verified; X = no defined ideal task. A never implies the action was completed.

Current outcome and reporting context

Road deaths

297 in 2019 (baseline), 239 in 2024 and 261 in 2025. The plan targets 148 by 2030.

The 2025 official progress table is dated 11 March 2026. Deaths increased in 2025 compared with 2024. This is a descriptive trend, not attribution of success or failure to a particular coordination activity.

Implementation reporting

The 2024 annual report recorded 62.11% realization of planned activities for that year. The 2025 report also records programme expenditure and performance indicators; definitions and time scope should be checked before making cross-year spending comparisons.

Lessons for the executive taskforce checklist

Practices worth retaining

Government decision and ministry lead; cross-sector working group with named members; assigned policy activities and phases; funding-proposal function; published monitoring and evaluation requirements.

Gaps to test explicitly

One accountable owner per action when multiple institutions are named; formal finance ministry representation; documented local consultation; evidence that integration projects became operational; actual delivery, expenditure and independently interpreted casualty outcomes.

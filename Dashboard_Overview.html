How the dashboard is organized

The Atlas gives a geographical inventory of initiatives. Taskforces presents coordinating bodies and membership evidence. Existing documents lists strategies, programmes and reports. Bodies & Strategies investigates which coordinating body is demonstrably connected to which output. Taskforce checklist defines 17 domains and 72 interventions. Country case studies documents specific institutional responsibilities and their sources.

The directories address different questions and should not be treated as a single uniform verification scale. A country's plan does not automatically establish which council authored it.

Research status and important boundary

This is not a certified 27-mechanism checklist audit. The available source-pass evidence covers 20 outstanding mechanisms, of which 36 checklist matches are documented. The seven earlier detailed country audits were not available as editable row-level records during this packaging session; this package does not reconstruct or replace their classifications. Most intervention cells for the 20 mechanisms still need individual verification.

Where source evidence is insufficient, the record indicates that further review is needed. Planned activities, reported implementation and verified impact are treated separately. None of the listed figures should be interpreted as a country performance ranking.

What to review before publication

Confirm the latest legal instruments, responsible institutions, strategy adoption dates, programme delivery evidence and source-level checklist matches. See the dedicated Audit Status, Source Matches, Intervention Matrix and Cross-Tab Review Queue data files in the downloadable package.

How to read the scores

F: Formal representationA government body or organization has a documented seat or statutory portfolio connection.
P: Partner or partial linkFor representation, there is a narrower functional connection. For interventions, only part of the defined responsibility is documented.
A: Exact planned responsibilityThe plan specifically matches the checklist intervention. This does not establish that the activity was carried out.
N/A and X: Not verified or not scoredN/A means the evidence was insufficient. X marks 13 matrix cells without a fully defined ideal intervention, excluded from the scoring.

Important: A planned action is not a completed action. Membership is not evidence of implementation. A change in road fatalities is not automatically caused by the coordinating body. The 22 detailed mechanisms are a selected sample and are not a census or ranking of 195 countries.

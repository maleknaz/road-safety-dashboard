How to interpret the directory

Results are grouped by country. Only named publications supported by a document-specific public link, a documented institutional listing, or a checked public source are included. A country without such evidence appears once, as “No verified document identified.”

Public document linkA document file or publication page is provided. Some links were supplied by the original inventories and have not been independently reopened.
Existence evidenced, no public copyAn official announcement, archive, or other documentary reference identifies the publication, but a document-specific public copy was not established.
No verified document identifiedNo qualifying named document was established in the evidence reviewed. This does not mean the country has no policies, laws, or strategies.

What is included?

National, provincial/state and local documents; adopted, historical and draft records may all appear if identifiable. A period such as “2021–2030” is not necessarily the publication date.

Issuing institution

The named publisher or issuing authority, not automatically the road-safety council associated with the document.

Evidence source

A source confirming the document or its publication. This is distinct from a direct link to the document itself.

Compilation date: 9 November 2026. Information combines the country policy inventory and institution-publication inventory with targeted source checks. This is a research catalogue, not a certification of the completeness or current legal status of any country's road safety policy framework. Independent document checks and original-inventory references are distinguished within each document record. No logos are reproduced.

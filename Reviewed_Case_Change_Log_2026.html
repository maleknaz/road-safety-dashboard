Data provenance and outstanding work

1. Previous reviews. Scores for 15 profiles are carried forward from user-reviewed case discussions and their outputs; detailed row-level source files could not all be mounted here. Do not present that material as independently re-audited in this package.
2. Available audits. All 17 domain rows and all 85 intervention rows were loaded directly from the Croatia, Cyprus and Estonia revision CSVs and were checked against their scores.
3. Two comparisons excluded from aggregation. Sweden’s wider delivery network is context, not an additional GNS case. The integrated executive system of Uganda is not a substitute for the PAFROS legislative forum.
4. Cohort boundary. Costa Rica remains provisionally included under the existing policy-instrument inclusion rule; a strict whole-of-government strategy rule may require reclassification after evidence review.
5. Unfinished Czechia update. The Czechia mechanism has only a first-pass comparison. No new F/P split or cell-level results are invented; retain its provisional 11/17 and 1 A, 5 P, 66 N/A until a source-based audit is completed.
6. Deployment boundary. The live Streamlit application cannot be modified simply by generating files here. Add the generated HTML files to your repository and replace app.py with the supplied drop-in navigation to publish the tabs.
Scope: previously discussed mechanisms only. Source material and revision notes are attributed inside each case. Prepared as an evidence-led research interface, not as official UN, UNITAR, or national government endorsement.

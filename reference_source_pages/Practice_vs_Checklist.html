What is compared, and what is not?

1. Who is at the table?

Seventeen government or economic-activity domains derived from a research crosswalk using COFOG/ISIC and road safety governance literature. A formal link (F) means a named seat or institution; a partner (P) means a narrower documented contribution. Unknown representation is not evidence of absence.

2. What responsibility was assigned?

Five Safe System pillars by 17 domains yield 85 cells; 72 specify an actionable ideal intervention and 13 are deliberately excluded. Exact (A) is a documented full planned match, partial (P) a narrower match, and N/A insufficient evidence.

3. Did it happen and was it effective? That is a separate outcome evaluation. A policy commitment, ministry membership, independently implemented partner project or strategy draft must never be misrepresented as delivery or causal impact.

This page summarizes the 22 mechanisms selected for this detailed research review, not all 27 mechanisms in the underlying directory. Full 85-cell source audits are provided in the downloadable research datasets for Croatia, Cyprus, Estonia, France, Japan, Jordan and Latvia; previously reported revisions for other cases are shown with provenance labels. Czechia and selected initial mechanism scores remain provisional.

Patterns worth translating into taskforce design

Separate approval from delivery

Bulgaria, Brazil, Canada and Croatia require distinct records for approval, coordination, implementation ownership and evaluation.

Distinguish a forum from a system

Sweden’s GNS and Uganda’s PAFROS cannot be credited with all national executive programmes.

Reconcile formal and operational partners

Chile, Cambodia, Costa Rica and Cyprus show why council membership and downstream technical involvement need separate verification.

Read the action annex

Croatia, Cyprus and Northern Ireland became substantially more specific once their official action tables and accountable actors were examined.

Make technical groups accountable

Brunei, Cyprus and Canada show specialized committees, but their powers and reporting requirements differ.

Flag policy comparability

Costa Rica’s institutional plan is substantively different from an approved whole-of-government national strategy.

How to use this toolkit

Explore country findings

Review documented institutions and their roles, inspect source links, and ask questions of the indexed country evidence.

Benchmark internationally

Compare research coverage across countries. These are evidence-coverage comparisons, not rankings of road safety performance.

Compare with another country

Select a peer to see side-by-side institutional evidence and differences by domain, with links to supporting sources.

Generate my institutional checklist

Answer optional questions, receive a research-prefilled 72-intervention checklist, and identify follow-up actions to verify gaps.

How the intelligence works. The toolkit retrieves source-linked records for the selected country, rather than beginning a new web investigation. When AI access is configured, you can request citation-checked analysis and limited searches for newer official information. Missing evidence and user responses remain unverified until reviewed.

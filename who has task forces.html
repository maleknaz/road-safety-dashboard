How to interpret the information

Institutions may be national councils, interministerial commissions, provincial or local task forces, and other coordination mechanisms. Some are historical or proposed, and multiple entries can refer to the same body.

  • Institution identified: a named coordinating body appears in the information reviewed. This is not a claim that its full membership, administrative hierarchy or current activity has been verified.
  • Unverified entity: the information reviewed was insufficient to establish a qualifying coordinating body. This is not proof that the country has no such institution.
  • Participating entities unverified: the exact ministries, agencies or organizations belonging to that particular body could not be confirmed. This appears as a clearly worded label on the institution, and the institution-level filter finds these entries across all countries.
  • Participating organizations: names are shown in English and the original language where recorded. Lists may be complete, partial, historical or unverified. The exact membership evidence note for each body should be read alongside the list.
  • Responsible office and hierarchy: the administrative home or reporting chain where established. Some bodies sit under a ministry, parliament, the office of a head of government, or an intergovernmental structure. Missing levels are not inferred.
  • Country counts: count entries, not necessarily unique active councils or commissions. The 24 countries with no confirmed body are included in the directory and can be opened for details.
  • Supporting links: references available for further checking. Missing links or incomplete details indicate limitations in the available information, not proof that relevant sources do not exist.
Road safety institutional coordination · Country directory. This directory brings together institutional descriptions, participating organizations, administrative relationships and supporting references. The evidence varies in completeness and may include historical or proposed arrangements. It is intended as a research and navigation resource, not confirmation of current legal or operational status.
